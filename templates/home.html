About The System

Why Pre-Travel Verification Matters

International border control and airline regulations have become significantly more stringent. Every year, thousands of passengers face denied boarding at departure gates due to overlooked requirements—such as passports expiring in under six months, unverified visas, lack of onward exit tickets, or absent travel medical insurance.

The AI-Based Pre-Travel Readiness Prediction System provides travellers and travel coordinators with a streamlined, intelligent pre-flight evaluation. By feeding key travel credentials through a supervised Decision Tree Classifier, the system identifies missing prerequisites in seconds and equips you with actionable remediation advice.

Features

Complete Pre-Departure Travel Coverage

Comprehensive multi-point validation to ensure full compliance before heading to the airport.

Passport Validity Verification

Evaluates passport expiry against your flight departure date to enforce the mandatory international 6-month rule.

Visa Availability Check

Verifies approved visas, eVisa validity, and visa-free travel eligibility for your specified destination country.

Return Ticket Verification

Ensures proof of return or onward exit travel required by border control to deter illegal overstay.

Hotel Booking Confirmation

Validates lodging reservations or host accommodation documentation for immigration arrival cards.

Travel Insurance Verification

Confirms active emergency medical and trip protection policies mandatory for Schengen, UAE, and others.

Vaccination Status Check

Audits destination-mandated immunization compliance including Yellow Fever, Polio, and COVID-19 protocols.

AI-Based Readiness Prediction

Employs a trained Scikit-learn Decision Tree model to classify travel preparedness with high precision.

Automated Report Generation

Generates BriefIQ-style AI summaries and downloadable PDF documentation with checklists and advice.
