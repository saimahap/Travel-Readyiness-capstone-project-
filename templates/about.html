Project Motivation & Problem Statement

International air travel is regulated by strict bilateral and multilateral entry protocols. Passengers arriving at airline check-in counters with unfulfilled prerequisites (e.g. less than six months remaining on their passport, absence of an exit ticket, or pending visa authorizations) face immediate denied boarding.

This capstone project introduces an automated decision-support framework that bridges the information gap for travelers, delivering instant document audits, predictive risk classification, and customized corrective steps.

Methodological Note & Verification Disclaimer: This system employs a supervised Machine Learning (Decision Tree) classifier to predict overall travel preparedness based on user-supplied travel parameters. The model output evaluates logical consistency and compliance rules. It does not claim to cryptographically authenticate actual government passport chips, visa stickers, or airline PNR records without external consular API integration.
